One vertical axis. Three storage levels.

The JetMax is mounted on the moving platform—not beside it. A box rides on a tray during lift travel. At the selected shelf, the arm transfers it between the tray and rack. Retrieval reverses the process and delivers the box to the bottom collection station.

What the PLC owns

Closed-loop lift positioning from an analog height sensor; upper/lower limits and digital shelf alignment; lift-drive commands; and ready/busy/done signals with the robot controller. The HMI requests a shelf and operation. SCADA records position, inventory changes, completed cycles, and faults.

Concept animation—not a hardware model. Timing and travel are illustrative. Payload, lift braking, arm reach, position sensing, guarding, and PLC-to-JetMax communication require validation. Each restart resets the simulated inventory.
